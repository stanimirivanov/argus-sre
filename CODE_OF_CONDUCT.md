# Code of conduct

## TL;DR

- Treat contributors and users with dignity, patience, and professional care.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and disclosure of private
  information are not acceptable.
- Report conduct concerns privately whenever possible.

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers commit to a welcoming, safe, and equitable
environment for people acting in good faith regardless of identity, background,
experience, ability, or viewpoint. This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).

## Expected and unacceptable behavior

Expected behavior includes empathy, constructive technical disagreement,
respect for boundaries, responsible handling of mistakes, and attention to the
effect of words and actions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory
or sexualized language or attention, sustained disruption, personal attacks,
retaliation, and publication of private information without permission.

## Scope, reporting, and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere. Report concerns privately to a maintainer using a private
contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile. If none is available,
request one publicly without identifying people or describing the incident.

Maintainers MUST respond impartially and preserve confidentiality as far as
practical. Proportionate actions may include removing content, correction,
warning, temporary limits, or permanent exclusion. An involved maintainer MUST
recuse themselves when another suitable reviewer is available. Retaliation is
prohibited.
